the DME doesn't control the speedometer - the instrument cluster does that. the DME does read the signal from the DSC module and interpret it, but it doesn't send the 5% higher signal to the cluster, the cluster adds that itself.

it's always the rear left sensor IIRC (might be rear right, but either way it's only one sensor, not an average).
