I had been sitting on a 3 stage IM with new DISAs (not OEM, the other brand available) for more than a year. A few months ago my starter died, as I had to remove the intake I had put the 3 stage one with the DISAs. Have been driving like that couple of months thinking one day I will do the flash from BimmerLabs. Well finally today I got to do it, and just took it for a spin and came back. And there is definite difference! I didn't have opportunity to test the upper range but low end torque difference is obvious. My car pulls at 6th gear same as it used to at 5th gear on high way. The other thing I noticed, the 325 I had was definitely suppressed in terms of performance. Now it is like the bans are removed. In neutral if I had to get the RPMs up previously I had to stab the pedal and stay on it. Like for double clutching, something was off. It is not in gear, there is nothing holding the engine to rev up, but it wasn't before. Now, it DOES! In neutral once it reaches 3K RPM it very quickly climbs up to 6K RPM.
The engine sound also seemed to have changed, which is probably due to intake paths are not different with the DISA's opening and closing.
The most obvious is something was holding back the engine before, that is removed now!
