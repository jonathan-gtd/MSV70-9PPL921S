WHOA... Thanks for the support guys, for sure. And for the legal advice.. I think?

The free "tune" isn't really just a "stock" tune. There's no such thing as a stock 330i tune for an MSV80 DME. We created it, based on our proprietary knowledge of these DMEs (I guarantee you nobody has come close to mapping as many parameters on these as we have). While it's essentially "stock" it's not just a file we copied from BMW. And we made a way you can flash it using all stock OEM software (WinKFP).

If you think you're getting some sort of guarantee or warranty with anything that's free, well, sorry - none is expressed or implied. However, I don't see there being any serious issues with the tunes (CELs? Even if you did get one, I could easily delete it). It should and will run like it was a 330i from the factory. The whole point is we know how to make it run like it was completely stock.

As far as being a not for profit org, well, that's not really true - but basically, we aren't at the point yet where we can offer a usable service that we can charge money for. Not to mention, the sellers rules on this forum mean I would be banned if I started advertising stuff for sale without a merchant account (which I have not done, and am not planning on doing any time soon).

The whole point of the free "tunes" is people would get to see what the Bimmerlabs platform can offer, as well as adding a new option that didn't exist before for 3 stage swaps (you could do the whole thing for under $250). Eventually we will be at a point where we can offer commercial tuning (or the ability to do so yourself). We are not there yet.

As for what's in our best interest, I guarantee we've put far more into this than we've gotten back so far. The reality is, we can already do "real" tunes now - and not just for MSV70 or MSV80. But I'm not going to just give those away for free, nor should we be expected to - still, one of the goals is to lower the barriers to tuning on BMWs (a primary one which is cost). I think we will achieve that. When we have a viable way to securely distribute the tunes (in the works), then we will have something to announce.

For those who have gotten tunes, or have contributed to us - thanks. For those who have not, don't be offended - it's mainly just due to proximity and logistics. I'd be happy to flash anyone's car for free who can drive to my house and chat about BMWs for 45 minutes, but there's only so many people who can realistically do that..

Otherwise, there's nothing much more to add, except I've been putting 20-40 hours a week into this for more than a year, on top of my day job. It won't be fruitless.
