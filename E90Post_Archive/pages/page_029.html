Originally Posted by rjahl
Curiosity got the best of me.

I pulled the power on the Valvetronic motor and used an Allen key on the motor to run to the eccentric cam to the physical stops. Using INPA on the laptop I recorded the following:

Bottom : 0.4 degrees
Top : 175.3 degrees

I was gentle on the Allen key and stopped when I felt and heard the cam hit the limits.

Not believing the results, I reset the VVL adaptions and tried again. Same numbers.

About 25 turns from top to bottom.

That about sums it, if you want more than 9.7MM valve lift you need to modify something. If there is space on the eccentric cam maybe you can file down the physical stops in the head but who knows what king of tuning nightmare that might cause.


EDIT: I went back to my graphs using the 175.4 degree number and came up with some additional clarifications on the valve lift

172 Degees = 9.72MM
175.4 Degrees = 9.88MM physical limit
178 Degrees - 10.01MM
180 Degrees = 10.1MM

So BMW did not completely lie about the 9.9 MM lift allowable in the Valvtronic II system. You just need to run the eccentric cam against the stops. LOL.
what's really interesting to me is most tuners are setting the lift map to 10mm. I guess nobody thought to look at what the actual lift/eccentric shaft angle is. When I dynoed that change, it made no perceptible difference, and it is now obvious why - because lift didn't change at all!

I'm going to focus on something that can actually be accomplished and that will make more power at high RPM - retarding the intake cam. Just need an excuse to pull my valve cover.

BTW, I'm finally making my header board for hooking up a BDM module while the DME is still in the car. It's basically an improved version of the "held together by glue and tape" version that I was using before it shorted out. It requires making a notch in the cover of the DME for the ribbon cable to pass through, or I'm also thinking of making a hole in the cover that you can just plug in the ribbon cable (this would be better as it would eliminate the possibility of the cable getting cut and shorting out). I'll end up with like 36 of them once the PCBs are printed out.
