Msv 80 partial xdf
i used this with some success to tune my own car.

v11_XDF_MSV80_0049QK0MI20S.zip

Built it using an MSV70 XDF and relative bin, and trying to find exact matches for msv80 bin data (endian swap and all), and some euristics...

I was helped by chatGPT, built me some (many) python scripts and something i did by eye

Not nearly complete, but all the good stuff is there:

- lambda full load and part load
- cat heating routines
- idle control
- rev limiters
- ignition logic
- vanos targets
- disa

and a lot of other stuff

DISCLAIMER: i used this and tuned my car with success, but i do not guarantee you will too. i used a sw tool for flashing, called MSV80Flasher, about 30 dollars or such. It does RSA delete and checksums, works 9 out of 10 times but when bricked i always was able to repair using same tool and reflash

PS: if someone wants to take it and add some more stuff (objective would be to map every MSV70 parameter in this MSV80 xdf if possible) - feel free to do it but SHARE IT

at the moment it looks like ball was dropped on MSV80 mapping

some stuff that i discovered, to be taken with a grain of salt

- milvs tuning escapes me - there is no need to alter any model from what i see, and changing targets for the eccentric shaft gains nothing. I never seen any indicator that the car isn't managing by itself the slightly more airmass

- car runs pig rich over 6k in stock rom

- car doesn't like a lot of ignition advance around 6k, tried to play a bit with the logic and by my timed logs there was no difference between mean 23° adv and mean 26° adv in the 4.5k to 7k band, so kept stock ignition

- car likes to pull timing for no reason, may be mine does: resolved lowering knock preamp for some cells

- going richer than 0.92 - 0.90 is useless

- best gain to get is removing the soft limiter logic / retuning it

- played with vanos targets, only gain I had (almost noise, not 10 hp) was retarding exhaust with a ramp that touches -115 at 7k

- no need to touch the torque model if NA, i see no intervention even if actual tq from model goes somewhat over commanded, no closure of valvetronic or throttle, no ign pull

these considerations are from tuning an euro 330i base software, the famous "factory mafless" one
