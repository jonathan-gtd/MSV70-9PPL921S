This is a bit inconclusive, but I came up with an idea of simply using stock 330i tune from bimmerlabs.

I am pretty sure MSV80 330i did not exist in the US, being replaced by 328i and 335i. I managed to find a 2010 E92 330i (258hp) for sale in my region, EU spec, and used its vin in bimmerlabs, downloaded the stock tune (8608462) for that car and flashed it.

Guess what? DMTL and 2FA3 code went away and car runs even better than the 328i to 330i tune (Which I had to change the powerclass byte myself otherwise the car would go into limp mode).

In short, bimmerlabs US 328i VIN RSA delete + EU 330i stock tune = working EU 325i to 330i engine swapped car.

And another added-bonus, I added VO $199 (no catalytic converter and leaded fuel, according to forum posts) in NCS Expert and coded DME, my hollowed out factory headers no longer throw code, so I guess it's success for my poor man's straight pipes.

(P.S.1: I am going to run the car for a while and see if it works as 330i, but I am probably eventually going to get a custom tune for $450 at 22RPD and actual headers, which again is kind of a shame bimmerlabs isn't "commercially" active and there isn't much community tuning/map editing out there)

(P.S.2: I flashed everything with a generic green PCB cable that I got in 2017, RSA delete took ~22 minutes and 0da ~1 minute, have a bimmergeeks cable on order but got impatient.)

In summary of my research and trials so far, I believe:

bimmerlabs 328i to 330i tune is essentially 330i map + powerclass change (powerclass set to 1 but DME ignores it). This requires RSA delete as the files are altered.

For someone like me swapping N52B30 into 325i, which is already powerclass 2, I believe I could have simply flashed stock 330i 0da and it would work without RSA delete, since it's original BMW file.





AKA: if you are doing 28i to 30i, I suggest you could try flashing a stock 30i tune (that's same region as yours) but you would have to change the powerclass byte, cheerio had some info on this and I simply use the address they found for the E90 (0x219 if i remember correctly). Otherwise you could try using their script to find the byte location and change it yourself.
